Favorite airports

    The filled house is home: the display centers on it. Click another house to move it there. The badge is the airport's flight category, from its latest METAR.

      Favorites are drawn in magenta and always labeled. Pinned: its track stays on the map when it is not selected. Beside each, where it was last seen. Suggestions are aircraft on the map or seen lately; anything else can be typed in full.

      Measuring...
      {% if can_clear %}

      Maps and aircraft photos are used for 30 days and then fetched afresh. Together the caches may take up to 32 GB, or half the free room on the card if that is less. Clearing removes downloaded maps, weather, airspace and aircraft details; they load again as needed. Settings, favorites and your own pictures are not touched.
